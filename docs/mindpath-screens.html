Four screens drawn at phone size: sign in, home, a sorting task, and the result. Calm, muted, patient-facing — no red alerts, no streak that can break, one clear action per screen.

What makes it work

Hand these tokens and rules to Claude Code and ask it to match the screens exactly, rather than describing the look in prose again.

Type

  • Fraunces for greetings, questions and results — warm, human, never clinical.
  • Hanken Grotesk for everything else: labels, body, buttons, numbers.
  • Display 31 · Title 25 · Body 14 · Caption 11.5. Nothing in between.

Shape & depth

  • Radius 26 on heroes, 22 on cards, 14 on badges, pill on buttons.
  • One soft shadow, tinted green, never grey.
  • Two ambient circles per screen at 7% — depth without clutter.
  • Concentric arcs are the brand motif: a fingerprint, a ripple settling.

Rules for this app

  • No red anywhere except the SOS chip.
  • No notification badges or counters — they feed checking compulsions.
  • No streak that resets to zero; count check-ins instead.
  • Progress is always visible: step X of Y, and time remaining.
  • Every screen has exactly one dominant action.
